## Check activity history

Tools → Utilities keeps a history of downloads and background operations.
If something seems to have failed silently, check here first.

## Common issues

- **Device not detected**: reconnect the device and confirm it's formatted
  for OPL/USBExtreme use.
- **Download stuck**: check your network connection; downloads are resumable
  and can usually continue after a retry.
- **Game shows `fragmented` or `needs_attention`**: see
  [Understanding Fragmentation & Repair](/oplforge/docs/understanding-fragmentation-repair/).

## Still stuck?

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/theguitarvity/src-app-oplforge/issues)
with as much detail as you can (OS, device type, and the activity history
entry if relevant).
