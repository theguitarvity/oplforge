## Why prepare a device?

OPL expects a specific folder structure on the storage device
(`DVD`, `CD`, `PS1`, `APPS`, `ART`, `CFG`, `VMC`). Preparing a device creates
this structure automatically so imported games and artwork land in the
right place.

## Steps

1. Connect the storage device.
2. Open the **Devices** hub and select the device.
3. Choose **Prepare device**.
4. OPL Forge creates the required folders and reports the result.

## After preparing

Once prepared, the device is ready for [library imports](/oplforge/docs/managing-your-library/)
and will show up with live space/health metrics on the Home screen.
