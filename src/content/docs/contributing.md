## Reporting issues

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/theguitarvity/src-app-oplforge/issues)
to report bugs or request features. Include your OS, device type, and
reproduction steps.

## Contributing code

OPL Forge is MIT-licensed and open to contributions. Fork the repository,
make your changes, and open a pull request describing what changed and why.

## Project status

OPL Forge currently has a single maintainer. Response times may vary —
thank you for your patience and for helping the project grow.
