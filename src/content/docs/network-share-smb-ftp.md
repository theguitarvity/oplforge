## What it does

The Network Share feature exposes your prepared library over SMB and FTP,
so a PS2 on the same local network can browse and launch games directly —
no need to physically move the storage device between the PS2 and your
computer.

## Setting it up

Enable the share from **Settings → Network**, then point your PS2's network
boot/browse tool at your computer's address on the local network.

## Security note

The share is intended for trusted local networks only. Do not expose it
directly to the public internet.
