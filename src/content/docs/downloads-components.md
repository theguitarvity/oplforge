## OPL Components

The Tools → OPL Components area (previously called "Essentials") manages
the binaries OPL needs to run, along with the PCSX2 compatibility database.

## Legal confirmation

Before any component is queued for download, OPL Forge shows a per-item
legal confirmation step — nothing downloads silently or without disclosure.

## Resilient downloads

Downloads use a durable queue with staging and a single writer per device,
so an interrupted download can resume instead of corrupting your device's
data.
