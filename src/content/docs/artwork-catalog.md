## Remote catalog search

The Catalog view searches remote metadata and cover-art sources (such as
Internet Archive) so you can find accurate information for your games.

## Applying artwork

Selected artwork is synced into the `ART` folder structure on your prepared
device, matched to the corresponding game entry.
